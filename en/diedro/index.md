**On iPhone Duo, the hinge is the instrument**: open the phone against a corner
and read the angle between its two halves, with its exterior, its supplement
and the miter saw setting that joins two pieces.

On any other iPhone, Diedro is a bubble level, a corner gauge and a true-scale
ruler. Nothing the sensor cannot measure is simulated.

## On iPhone Duo

- **Square**: color and a tap tell you when you reach 90°, 45° and the usual
  workshop angles.
- **Target**: pick an angle and be guided to it. Use it to transfer an angle
  from one piece to another.
- **Two-plane level**, with the Duo folded.
- **Hold**: the reading freezes once it is steady, so you can read it calmly.
- Shut it, and the cover display keeps the last reading.

## On any iPhone

- **Bubble level** lying flat, on two axes, or on its edge, in degrees and
  percent of slope.
- **Corner gauge**: rest the iPhone on one face of a corner, then on the other.
- **True-scale ruler** in millimeters or inches.

On iPhone Duo, Diedro Pro unlocks the workshop tools; on other iPhones nothing
is locked.

## Honest about precision

Diedro shows the resolution the sensor really delivers, with no decorative
decimals. It is a handy instrument, not a certified one. No ads, no account,
no tracking.

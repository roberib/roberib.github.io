Your camera roll fills up with photos you never look at again: blurry,
duplicated, forgotten. Limina shows them to you one at a time and you decide
with a finger: **right to keep, left to let go**. Once you have decided on a
photo, it never comes back.

Nothing is deleted until you confirm at the end of a round, and whatever you
delete goes through Recently Deleted, where you can recover it for 30 days.

## Where to start

- **Oldest, newest or shuffled**, to work through the backlog.
- **Videos**, the biggest first (Pro).
- **Screenshots**, which hardly anyone means to keep.
- **Bursts**: the whole sequence on one card, instead of fifty shots of the
  same jump.
- **Similar**: near-identical shots taken seconds apart (Pro).
- **Live Photos**: keep the photo and reclaim the space its video takes (Pro).
- **A USB drive or SSD** plugged into your iPhone or iPad, with the same swipes
  (Pro).

Your favorites are skipped automatically, and infinite mode (Pro) goes from one
round to the next without stopping at the summary.

## Privacy

Your photos never leave the phone and there is no account. No ads and no
tracking, free or Pro.

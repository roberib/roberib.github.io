**The phone is the dial.** Open and close it to turn, listen as it gets louder
as you get close, and hold still on the click. Three numbers open a safe, and
the alarm is counting.

On any other iPhone you play by tilting it like a lid: lay it flat and lift the
top edge.

## The job

- **Six nights, room by room**: a keypad, a lock, the safe, the lasers, the
  guard's torch, a jammed door and the getaway.
- The first safe shows its numbers. From the second on they are hidden and you
  find them **by ear**, from the clicks in the last ten degrees.
- **Fourteen more ways in**, each a heist of its own, with the hinge or with
  your fingers.
- A ledger with the combination and the loot of every safe.
- Close the phone to lie low: the alarm waits until you are back.

Best with headphones, at night.

## No trace

No ads, no sign-in. Scores and progress stay on the phone; only a purchase is
checked online.

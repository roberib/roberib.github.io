Not all the "duplicates" in your camera roll are the same, and each kind is
cleaned up differently:

- **Copies**: the same photo or video saved twice, for example when you import
  it or it gets forwarded back to you.
- **Bursts**: the dozens of photos the camera fires off in one go.
- **Similar photos**: the five tries it takes by hand to get the group shot
  right.

Photos handles the first two well. The third, not at all.

## 1. Copies: the Duplicates collection

In the **Photos** app, go to **Collections › Utilities › Duplicates**. Each set
of copies appears together. Tap **Merge** on a set, then
**Merge [number] Items**.

According to Apple, merging keeps the highest-quality version, with the data
from all the copies, and moves the rest to **Recently Deleted**. If you have no
copies, the Duplicates collection does not even appear.

## 2. Bursts: keep only the good ones

Bursts live in **Collections › Media Types › Bursts**. Open one and tap
**Burst ([number])**. Gray dots below the thumbnails mark the photos iOS
suggests keeping. Tap the circle on each photo you want to keep, then **Done**.

iOS then asks what to do with the rest: choose **Keep Only [number]
Favorites** and the others go to Recently Deleted. A thirty-photo burst comes
down to two or three.

## 3. Similar photos: by hand

If they are not exact copies or a burst, Photos does not group them: to the
app they are different photos, even if you took them in the same second. You
have to go one by one: open them, swipe between them, heart the one worth
keeping, then use **Select** to delete the rest.

It is the most common case and the slowest, because you also have to find
them among thousands of photos.

## 4. Do not forget Recently Deleted

None of the above frees any space yet: everything goes to **Recently
Deleted**, where iOS keeps it for 30 days. To get the space back now, go to
**Collections › Recently Deleted** (under Utilities), unlock it with Face ID,
tap **Select**, and delete what you do not want.

<p class="note">Careful if you use iCloud Photos: what you delete on your
iPhone is also deleted on your other devices and in iCloud.</p>

## The shortcut: Limina

[Limina](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6804942727) is an app I made for
cleaning up your library with a swipe. For duplicates it has two modes:

- **Bursts** (free): it shows you each camera burst whole, with the photo
  Limina recommends marked with a star. Tap **Keep this one** on the ones you
  want and the rest are marked for deletion in one tap. If you want them all,
  **Keep the whole burst**.
- **Similar** (Limina Pro): it finds the near-identical photos you took by
  hand, the ones from the same second and the same place, and shows them to
  you just like a burst. That is exactly the case Photos does not cover.

To recommend a photo, Limina works out on the iPhone itself which shot came
out best, faces included. At the end of a round it tells you how much space
you are about to free, and nothing is deleted until you confirm. Everything
deleted goes to Recently Deleted, so there is always a way back.

What Limina does not do is merge exact copies: for that, the Photos Duplicates
collection already works well.

It all happens on your iPhone: no account, nothing uploaded anywhere, and no
ads.

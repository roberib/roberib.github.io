[Swipewipe](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1583884012) made swiping through
your camera roll popular: one way to keep, the other to delete. It works, and
nearly a hundred thousand ratings on the US App Store prove it. But there are
reasons to look elsewhere: its plans are mostly weekly subscriptions, its
privacy label includes data used to track you, or you are simply missing
something specific.

These are the alternatives I compared. Full disclosure up front: **the first
one, Limina, is mine**. That is why it comes first; the rest of the list tries
to be fair to each app.

<p class="note">Prices and privacy labels taken from the US App Store on
October 22, 2026. Apps change their plans often, so check before you pay.</p>

## At a glance

| App | How you pay | Data used to track you? | What sets it apart |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limina | $4.99/month or $29.99 once | No | Bursts, Live Photos and USB/SSD drives |
| Swipewipe | Mostly weekly ($3.99–$9.99), yearly $29.99 | Yes | Photo map and "On This Day" |
| Slidebox | Weekly, yearly or one-time | No | Sort into albums as you swipe |
| Slider | Yearly, $19.99 | Yes | Built with videos in mind too |
| Photo Cleaner ® | Weekly, monthly or yearly | Yes | Go through your library month by month |
| Cleanup | Mostly weekly | Yes | Duplicates, contacts, email and video compression |

## 1. Limina

[Limina](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6804942727) shows you your photos
fifteen at a time and you swipe to keep them or mark them. At the end of each
batch you see how much space you are about to free, and nothing is deleted
until you confirm. Once you have decided on a photo, it never shows it again.

- **Modes:** oldest, newest, shuffled, screenshots, "this time last year",
  whole bursts at once, similar photos, videos (longest first) and Live
  Photos, where you can keep just the photo and reclaim the space its video
  takes.
- **External drives:** plug a USB drive or SSD into your iPhone or iPad and
  clean it up with the same swipes. I have not seen another app on this list
  do that.
- **Privacy:** everything happens on your iPhone, with no account and no ads.
  Its App Store label only declares purchases, not linked to you.
- **Price:** free to review photos every day; Limina Pro (videos, similar
  photos, Live Photos, drives and infinite mode) is $4.99 a month or $29.99
  once.
- **Downsides:** it is new and barely has any ratings yet, and it needs a
  recent version of iOS.

## 2. Swipewipe

The reference in this genre. Besides swiping, it goes through your library
month by month, has "On This Day" with a widget and streaks, a map of where
you took each photo, and AI photo enhancement. Its US plans range from $3.99
to $9.99 a week, plus a $29.99 yearly plan, and its privacy label includes data
used to track you.

Do not confuse it with "Swipe Photo Cleaner: SwipeWipe", which is from a
different developer.

## 3. Slidebox

The veteran: it has been on the App Store since 2015. Its strength is that,
as you swipe, you can send each photo to an album, so it organizes as well as
cleans. It also compares similar photos and lets you undo. It offers weekly or
yearly subscriptions and a one-time purchase, and its privacy label has no
tracking.

## 4. Slider

Very similar gesture, with good video support and a review step before you
empty the trash. Its only listed purchase is a $19.99 yearly subscription. Its
privacy label includes data used to track you.

## 5. Photo Cleaner ®

It organizes your library by month and year: you open "September 2021" and
swipe through that month. It asks for confirmation before deleting. Plans are
weekly ($3.99), monthly ($7.99) and yearly ($29.99), and its privacy label
includes data used to track you.

## 6. Cleanup

Not a swipe app but an all-in-one cleaner: duplicates, similar photos with the
best one marked, video compression, duplicate contacts and even junk email.
It is by far the most used. Its listed plans are mostly weekly ($5.95 to
$11.99), and its privacy label includes data used to track you.

## Which one should you pick?

- **You want to pay once and be done:** Limina or Slidebox.
- **You do not want to be tracked:** Limina or Slidebox, going by their
  privacy labels.
- **You have photos on a USB drive or SSD:** Limina.
- **You also want to sort into albums:** Slidebox.
- **You also want to clean contacts or email:** Cleanup.
- **You like Swipewipe and its memories map:** stick with Swipewipe.
